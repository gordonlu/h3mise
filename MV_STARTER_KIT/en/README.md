# H3Mise MV Starter Kit

Start with a song, use H3Mise to manage the story, characters, shots, generation jobs, and review decisions, and finish an MV.

Everything in this folder is Markdown and can be copied to another person on its own. You do not need the author's project, assets, or scripts; bring your own song, lyrics, character images, and RunningHub account. It is an operating guide and copyable template, not an automatic project importer or one-click generator.

## Start here

1. Read [Quick start](01-quick-start.md) and complete a short narrative shot and a singing shot.
2. Add a narrative App and an original-audio lip-sync App following the [RunningHub workflows](02-runninghub-workflows.md).
3. Use the [singing prompts](03-singing-prompts.md) to keep the original vocal timing; use the [narrative prompts](04-narrative-prompts.md) to describe concrete action and space.
4. Copy the [project production sheet](05-project-production-sheet.md) and fill in your song, story, shots, budget, and review notes.
5. Finish the whole piece following [Review and delivery](06-review-and-delivery.md).
6. If you want an AI Agent to help, fill in the [task for an AI assistant](07-ai-agent-task.md) and give it to the assistant.

Keep the song under 3 minutes where possible, ideally around 2 minutes 30 seconds; validate a real sample before batch production. Prefer outputting the target 1080p directly at generation time and verify the actual dimensions of the artifact. A successful App connection recorded here does not mean native 1080p has been verified.

When you need to install the program, use it together with H3Mise. This folder can be read independently, and the launch requirements are also in Quick start.
