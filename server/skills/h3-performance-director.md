# H3 Performance Director (starter)

Performance, weight, physics and movement grammar for the subject in a single
continuous H3 shot.

## Performance beat
Every shot has: objective → obstacle → tactic → turn. State them explicitly.
The performance turn is the moment the subject commits to the action.

## Movement quality (Laban)
Describe the primary action with four qualities:
- weight (strong / light / neutral)
- time (sudden / sustained / neutral)
- space (direct / indirect)
- flow (bound / free)
Keep the vocabulary small and consistent across shots of the same character.

## Physics defaults (strict_realism)
- Anticipation precedes primary action; follow-through and recovery after.
- Ground contact: feet planted, no floating; weight transfers visibly.
- Inertia: heavy objects resist direction change; momentum carries through.
- Cause before effect; contact before reaction.
- Biology: joints bend naturally, locomotion respects gait and anatomy.
- Vehicles/aircraft: acceleration, banking, and drag read plausibly.

## Gaze & end pose
- Gaze leads action or reveals intention; state where the eyes go.
- The end pose is the frame the next shot must match (if continuity matters).
