# H3 Shot Pattern Library (starter)

Camera and spatial pattern guidance for single continuous H3 shots.

## Selection rules
- Establishing: wide, slow push-in, subject enters frame.
- Action: follow the motion owner; camera speed relates to subject speed
  (leading, trailing, or locked).
- Reaction: hold a medium/close shot; camera movement minimal, trigger after
  the subject's performance turn.
- Closeup: frame on gaze/primary action; movement quality soft, weighted.
- Insert: static or slow drift; geometry chosen to isolate the object.

## Movement syntax
- One dominant camera behavior per shot (push / pull / pan / tilt / orbit /
  static). Do not combine multiple strong behaviors in 5 seconds.
- Speed relation must be explicit: faster than / locked to / slower than the
  subject.
- Every camera behavior needs a trigger and a stop condition.
- Shot sizes evolve over time (start → peak → end); peak usually lands on the
  dramatic peak of the shot.

## Spatial readability
- Keep the subject on a readable movement axis; avoid reversing screen
  direction mid-shot.
- State geometry concretely (e.g. "low angle, dolly in on the axis, 24mm").
